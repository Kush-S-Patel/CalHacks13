## Strong vs weak

**We aim strong**

- Real messy corner + answer key (`claims/scenario.py`)
- Few roles done deep (Claims Agent + Finance Manager)
- Asks at the right moment; **handles a no** (interactive Priya card)
- Limits in code; record of every call
- Stage moment + Hyphenate score (`sow/scoring.py`)

**We reject**

- One-shot extractors / planted-flag dashboards  
- Scripted happy paths with no manager  
- Ten shallow ERP workflows as the pitch  
- Leading with the bakery short-wire workshop twin
