# SwarmCFO Claims — CalHacks 13 one-minute demo

## Audience and track fit
For the Hyphenate track: a real finance team’s messy corner, handled deeply by a system of work. The demo shows a claim agent ask for judgment at the right time, learn from a manager’s answer, improve the next month, enforce role limits in code, and prove each step in an append-only audit.

## Story in 60 seconds
1. Hook: on a $860 expense, the agent stops and asks.
2. Product: turn receipts and finance records into review-ready federal claims alongside existing systems.
3. Human judgment: Jordan asks Priya whether catering is program or staff spend.
4. Outcome: her answer clears the four exceptions and updates claim evidence.
5. Learning: Priya-approved policy holds a similar October expense before claim entry.
6. Trust: code denies an agent’s approval attempt; the audit records the full trace.
7. Close: one messy finance workflow, done deeply — learns, improves, proves.

The names and organization are the project’s demo scenario, not live customers. Screens are genuine saved captures from the project demo.
