# Brag Plan: SwarmCFO Claims — 60-second demo

## What is this app?
SwarmCFO Claims prepares federal grant reimbursements for outsourced bookkeepers, asks a finance manager to resolve an ambiguous expense, turns her answer into policy, and records the work in an audit.

## The angle
Open on the consequence: one $860 question in September becomes an automatic policy hold in October. Then show how the product gets there and why a finance manager can trust it.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
Start on real completed UI with a bold two-part headline: “ONE $860 QUESTION.” → “OCTOBER ALREADY KNOWS.” Reveal `HELD BY POLICY` immediately. Make the product and the consequence legible before explaining the workflow.

## Key moments
- Explain SwarmCFO’s narrow job: it works with the ledger and grant portal but does not change either or submit claims.
- Show Jordan stop on the $860 North Bay Catering ambiguity and ask Priya rather than guess.
- Show Priya’s exclusion update the claim, become a precedent and approved policy, hold a similar October charge, then block Jordan’s approval attempt while preserving the audit.

## Outro
Land the score, then end cold on “That’s the hire.”

## User flow worth showing
Live demo entry → Jordan pauses on the $860 charge → Priya excludes it → claim metrics and policy update → October repeat is held → Jordan is denied approval and the audit records why.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: calm, specific finance product demo with a hard, attention-grabbing outcome-first open.
- Interpretation: real UI remains the main picture; add a small number of large, high-contrast labels and a restrained narrated guide.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 60 seconds

## Visual identity
- Background: `#0c1210`
- Accent: `#3d9b6a`, bright accent `#5fd49a`, decision amber `#e0a045`
- Text: `#e8f0ea`
- Display font: Playfair Display / Georgia
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the actual Priya decision card, the before/after reimbursement result, and the append-only audit.

## Demo integrity
The names and organization in the UI are the project's demo scenario. Use the saved real screen recording and screenshots from the recorded scenario. Do not imply these are live customers or claim a live API run during this export.

## Share copy
One $860 question becomes a policy that holds the next month’s repeat. SwarmCFO Claims asks when it should, learns from a manager’s answer, and proves every action.

## Audio direction
- Role: clear, calm narration over restrained upbeat music.
- Voice: Kokoro `af_heart`, lightly accelerated to keep the concise narration inside one minute; narration is part of the user's supplied demo script.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, one-minute track.
- Music treatment: narration stays forward; keep music around 0.14 under the voice, fade in over 0.5s and out over the last 2s.
- Music cue guidance: use the bundled vol-10 cue preset as timing guidance only; reserve three strongest moments for the opening hook, the policy/October reveal, and the final “That’s the hire.”
- Audio-reactive treatment: none; narration and real UI carry the story.
- SFX posture: sparse; one soft button click, one policy hold accent, one quiet DENIED accent. Keep all below narration.
- Restraint rule: no buzzer or victory sound on DENIED; no SFX on every metric.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — October already knows — 5s
Use the real completed UI as a darkened full-frame field, then bring in the $860 question and October hold as a bold split headline. State the surprise immediately.
VO: “One $860 question in September. October: held by policy.”
Sequential/interaction: show September → October as one clear transformation, not a rapid text sequence.
Audio intent: hook lands at frame one; music starts under narration.
Transition mood: decisive cut → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — What the product does — 10s
Show the actual site moving from hero to the “How it works” section. Keep a brief label: `SYSTEM OF WORK · NOT THE PORTAL OR LEDGER`.
VO: “SwarmCFO Claims prepares federal reimbursement packages for outsourced bookkeepers. It works with the ledger and grant portal, but never changes them or submits a claim.”
Sequential/interaction: keep the real webpage capture moving slowly; do not cover its product explanation.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Jordan asks — 8s
Use the actual pending Priya decision capture. Frame the question and the exclusion button large enough to read; show the four-exception context and `$860` charge.
VO: “Jordan finds four exceptions. Is catering a program lunch—or a staff celebration? He asks Priya instead of guessing.”
Sequential/interaction: a subtle pointer moves to the real `No — staff celebration. Exclude it.` button and clicks once.
Audio-coupled idea: one quiet click aligns with the pointer action.
Transition mood: soft, then cut to the completed result → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — The claim changes — 10s
Cut to the real completed result capture. Show `$8,160` kept off award, `$124` receipt attached, `$28,135` eligible, and `4 → 0` exceptions as a readable, consistent metric row.
VO: “Priya says, ‘Exclude it.’ The claim changes: $8,160 kept off the award, a $124 receipt attached, $28,135 eligible. Four exceptions fall to zero.”
Sequential/interaction: metrics arrive in two pairs, then hold together.
Transition mood: confident → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — It carries forward — 9s
Stay on the real learning/policy/October proof UI. Highlight the precedent, policy approval, and October auto-hold in that order.
VO: “Her answer becomes a precedent, then policy Priya approves. Next month’s similar catering is held before it enters a claim.”
Audio-coupled idea: one quiet reveal accent as `HELD BY POLICY` appears.
Transition mood: deliberate → Scene 6.

### Scene 6 — Approval is a boundary — 9s
Use the real audit-trail capture. Enlarge the agent approval row and `DENIED`; show the manager approval and audit entries nearby.
VO: “Then Jordan tries to approve. Denied—in code. Only Priya can approve, and every question, answer, revision, and policy is in the audit.”
Audio-coupled idea: restrained low impact under `DENIED`, no alarm.
Transition mood: crisp → Scene 7.

### Scene 7 — That’s the hire — 9s
End on an uncluttered brand-and-score card, preserving the exact project metrics: `4 → 0 critical exceptions`, `0 unauthorized approvals`, and the claim outcomes. Hold the final sentence.
VO: “Zero unauthorized approvals. It learns, improves next month, and proves every call. That’s the hire.”
Transition mood: resolved end; let the last words land cold.

**Music mood:** upbeat but low and steady under narration.
**Audio summary:** narration is primary; three small cues mark the decision, policy hold, and denial, then music fades on the closing line.
