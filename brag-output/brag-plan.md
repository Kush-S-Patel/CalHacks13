# Brag Plan: SwarmCFO Claims

## What is this app?
SwarmCFO Claims prepares federal grant reimbursement packages for a bookkeeping team. It matches ledger lines and receipts, asks a finance manager about an ambiguous expense, learns a standing policy from the answer, and proves its actions in an append-only audit.

## The angle
A finance agent should not need to be told the same thing every month. In this demo, Priya's answer about one $860 catering charge becomes a reusable precedent and policy; the next month's repeat is held for a person. Jordan can prepare, ask and propose, but cannot approve or submit the claim.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Start on the real SwarmCFO Claims hero. The visual thought: “One no. October remembers.” The audience sees immediately that the product carries a correction into the next monthly claim.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Explain the product boundary: the bank, GL and receipts remain systems of record; SwarmCFO is the system of work for claims, decisions and audit. It does not replace the grant portal or alter the ledger.
- Show Jordan matching the ledger, surfacing four exceptions, then pausing to ask Priya about the ambiguous $860 catering line instead of guessing.
- Show “staff celebration — exclude it” become a precedent and policy. A matching October expense is auto-held; Jordan's approval attempt is denied and Priya retains approval.

## Outro / punchline
The product's objective result lands as a scorecard, then “One corner done deep.”

## User flow worth showing
Entry: real SwarmCFO Claims hero and its live September claim. Key action: Jordan stops on an ambiguous $860 catering expense and asks the finance manager. Result: Priya's answer becomes a precedent and policy, a similar October expense is held, the agent is denied approval, and the audit records the manager's approval.
